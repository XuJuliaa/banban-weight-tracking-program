BanBan Weight Tracker

A free, private tracker for your weight. It works in any browser, phones included, and can be installed like an app.

What it does

  • Weight: log one weight per day in kg or lb, and see your 10-day average, how regularly you log, and a chart of your daily weights.
  • Slope: see your current pace, the weight gained or lost per 30 days, from a straight-line fit over the previous 60 days.
  • Your files: download your data as a CSV file, or edit it directly in your own file.

Your data stays yours

The app has no server. Your entries are saved where you choose: in a "BanBan Weight Tracker" folder in your own Google Drive (as Google Sheets), or in a folder on your own computer. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Continue with Google asks for permission to use only the Google Drive files this app creates. The app can't see anything else in your Drive, and your data is never sent to us.
