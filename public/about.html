BanBan Weight Tracker

A free, private tracker for your weight, food and exercise. It works in any browser, phones included, and can be installed like an app.

What it does

  • Weight: log a weigh-in in kg or lb, and see your 10-day average, how regularly you log, and a trend chart with a trend line.
  • Food: log what you eat by category and see the mix as a pie chart.
  • Exercise: log cardio, strength and flexibility sessions and see your minutes per day.
  • Your files: download everything as a .zip of CSV files, or edit your data directly in your own files.

Your data stays yours

The app has no server. Your entries are saved where you choose: in a "BanBan Weight Tracker" folder in your own Google Drive (as Google Sheets), or in a folder on your own computer. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Continue with Google asks for permission to use only the Google Drive files this app creates. The app can't see anything else in your Drive, and your data is never sent to us.
